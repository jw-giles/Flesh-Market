*** RETIRED. THESE TEXTURES ARE NO LONGER SHIPPED OR USED. ***
The facade sheets were removed in v1.9.1.0 when the city moved to Voloshka's
modular kit. The reason is not a complaint about the art, which was good: a
canvas 2D renderer maps images through an AFFINE transform, a rectangle in
perspective is a PROJECTIVE map, and no amount of subdivision closes that gap -
so a photographic facade on a box always bowed and always gapped. Geometry
whose faces carry one baked colour has no such problem.

papptimus stays in docs/CREDITS.md and in the in-client Asset Credits panel.
CC BY attribution is owed for USE, the sheets were used for several releases,
and removing the credit because the files left the tree would be reading the
licence backwards.

The original record follows.

=====================================================================

FleshMarket - cyber city facade textures
========================================

WHAT THESE ARE
Nine albedo textures and their nine matching emissive maps, plus two roof
textures. 256x256 pixel-art facades: brick, shutters, glazing, doors, service
risers. The emissive maps are black except where a window, a sign or a keypad
is lit, which is exactly the split a night scene wants - the albedo is drawn
at the ambient value and the emissive is added on top so lit windows are
LIGHT SOURCES rather than bright paint.

They are drawn by client/assets/city-battle.js onto building facades. See
"HOW THEY ARE USED" below for why only part of the pack was taken.

CHANGES MADE (required to be stated under CC BY 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B))
- Only 20 of the pack's 29 texture files were taken: the nine albedo facades
  that have a matching emissive map, those nine emissive maps, and the two
  roof textures. Files taken verbatim, byte for byte, renamed not at all.
- Nothing was resampled, recoloured or recompressed. The renderer tints at
  draw time from the planet palette; the files on disk are the originals.
- The FBX meshes were NOT taken. See below.


WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT TAKEN, AND WHY
The pack ships twenty FBX meshes - four whole buildings and sixteen modular
sections. They are not here.

They are binary FBX (Kaydara FBX Binary, 2020), and this renderer has no
mesh format that could receive them without a converter being written first:
the nature pack went through tools/nature-meshes.py to become a flat vertex
and face list, and an FBX importer is a substantially bigger job than that
was. More to the point, the meshes are the part of the pack we need LEAST.
The building shapes in this scene are generated - footprint, height, setback,
cornice, roof plant - and they need to stay generated, because the block layout
places them procedurally against streets and cover slots. What was missing was
never the boxes. It was the SURFACES on the boxes, and that is what the
textures are.

If the meshes are wanted later, tools/nature-meshes.py is the pattern to copy
and an FBX-to-JSON step is the work; the licence permits it and this note is
the record that it was a decision rather than an oversight.

The five unpaired emissive maps (building_emiss, building3_emiss,
building10_emiss, building_10a_emiss, building_red_2_emiss) were left out
because they have no albedo in the pack to sit on; the renderer draws emissive
only over its own albedo, so an orphan map has nothing to be emissive OF. The
rail textures were left out because there is no rail in this scene yet.
