FleshMarket - modular low poly city kit
=======================================

WHAT THIS IS
client/assets/space/city/kit.json - 526 modular meshes baked from Voloshka's
"Industrial Low Poly City", with each face's palette colour and emissive colour
resolved at build time. Walls, 10 window types (lit / unlit / partly lit), 3
door designs, roof sections, stairs, road and pavement pieces, kerbs, crossings,
lamps, fences, signs, bins and bare trees, in 10 coordinated colour variants.

Regenerate with:
    python3 tools/city-meshes.py <path-to-IndustrialCityAsset>
    python3 tools/city-meshes.py <path-to-IndustrialCityAsset> --check

The source FBX and its two 1024px palette textures are NOT in this repository.
Only the derived kit.json is.

COMMERCIAL USE IS EXPLICITLY GRANTED. REDISTRIBUTION IS NOT ADDRESSED, and that
distinction is the one this repo keeps tripping over. "Free to use in your
projects" is a licence to USE; it does not say the source art may be published
in a public repository for anyone to take. That is why the FBX and the palette
PNGs are not here and only the derived kit.json is - the same position the
nature pack settled into, and the same one ATTRIBUTION.txt in the terrain
directory argues for at length.

If the repository is public and you want to be certain, ask Voloshka directly.
It is one message and the answer is probably yes.

*** THE PAID SCENE IS DELIBERATELY NOT USED. *** IndustrialCityScene.zip is the
$5 support edition - a pre-assembled demo city - and its own Credits.txt says
"thank you for buying". Nothing from it is baked or shipped. The free modular
kit is what this game builds from, which is also the better engineering answer:
a kit is assembled procedurally per colony, and a fixed demo scene would be one
city on sixty-six worlds.

THE POSITION IS THE SAME AS THE KIT ABOVE, and for the same reason. A purchase
buys use. It does not, by itself, say anything about publishing the source art
in a repository anyone can clone, and this page says nothing at all - so only
the DERIVED mesh data is here and the FBX is not. If this repository is public,
one message to the author settles it.

WHAT WAS NOT TAKEN: the trains, helicopters, tanks, forklifts and racking. They
are good models and this is a city battlefield, not a rail yard, and every one
of them is 1,500 to 4,000 faces. If a tank is ever wanted, it is already baked
by tools/city-meshes.py --materials and only the shipping subset excludes it.
