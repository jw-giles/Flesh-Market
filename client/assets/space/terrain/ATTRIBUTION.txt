FleshMarket - battlefield ground patches
========================================

WHAT THESE ARE
Fourteen 512x512 greyscale textures, two per COLONY_VISUAL terrain key
(<key>_base.png and <key>_rock.png). Each is an 8x8 arrangement of 64px
seamless top-down tiles, flipped and blended, then reduced to luminance.
They are tiled by the client with a repeating canvas pattern and given
their colour at runtime from client/assets/planet-palette.js, so the same
patch serves a red desert, a teal rift and an ice world.

They are BAKED, not hand-authored. Regenerate with:

    python3 tools/terrain-patches.py <path-to-tile-pack>
    python3 tools/terrain-patches.py <path-to-tile-pack> --check

The tile pack itself is deliberately NOT in this repo. Only these derived
patches are.

GRASS IS NOT FROM THE PACK. IT IS GENERATED.
------------------------------------------
grass_base.png and grass_rock.png are NOT baked from any tile pack. They are
produced by tools/terrain-synth.py out of arithmetic: wrapping value-noise
lattices for the clumping, aligned blade strokes whose direction comes from a
low-frequency lean field, and a bare-scrape mask. No source art, no pack, no
licence to resolve.

TWO REASONS THEY EXIST. The pack below has no grass in it at all - the recipe
is sand, gravel, basalt, snow, water and clay - so a Coalition garden world was
drawing its fields with dust_base tinted green, which is gravel in a costume.
And the pack's licence is still unread, so anything derived from it is
unlicensed. A generated patch fixes both at once and is the only one of these
files that can ship today without a decision being made first.

They are also authored WIDE: measured, the tinted dust patch spans luminance 41
to 65, a 24-value range, which is why a plain reads as a flat mat however much
fine noise is on it. These span roughly twice that, so the crush in
buildPatterns has something left to crush.

Regenerate and verify with:

    python3 tools/terrain-synth.py
    python3 tools/terrain-synth.py --check

--check asserts the shipped PNGs are byte-identical to what the generator
produces, that the tile wraps exactly (the wrap difference must be
indistinguishable from an ordinary neighbouring-column difference), and that
the range has not narrowed.

THE PATTERN, WHICH IS NOW THE ACTUAL PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------
Three of the four art assets in this game cannot legally sit in a public
repository, and the one that can is CC0. That is not bad luck with three
packs; it is what nearly every commercial and semi-commercial art licence
says. "Use it in your game, do not redistribute the art" is the norm.

Fixing this asset by asset will keep failing. The structural options are:

  a. Make the repository private. Cheapest, and it costs the public repo.
  b. Keep the repo public and move client/assets/space/{terrain,vehicles,
     brood} out of git entirely - gitignored, deployed to the VPS by a
     separate rsync step in ship.sh. The renderer already degrades to
     wireframe when art is absent, so a clone without the art still runs.
  c. Replace everything with CC0 or self-owned art, as the nature pack
     already is.

(b) is the one that keeps both the public repo and the art, and the
fallback behaviour to make it work is already built.

work and the whole battlefield colour scheme goes with it.
